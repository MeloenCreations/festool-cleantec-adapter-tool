How it locks

Push the male CLEANTEC plug on the hose into the socket and twist it until the three lugs sit behind the shelves. Pulling straight out will not release it. Twist back to the entry slots to remove it.

Printing

  • Open the file in OpenSCAD, render it with F6, and export an STL.
  • Print with the tube on the bed and the socket mouth facing up. The shelves are chamfered so they do not need supports.
  • PETG is a good default. Use three or four perimeters so the wall stays airtight.
  • If the plug will not turn, raise lip_deep_r and lip_shallow_r in the file by 0.1 mm or 0.2 mm.
