How the top ends work

CLEANTEC: push the male plug on a Festool hose into the socket and twist until the three lugs sit behind the shelves. Twist back to the entry slots to remove it.

Dyson V6: slide a Dyson tool onto the male tip until the latch clicks into the groove. The four flats let the tool sit in any 90° orientation.

Tube: a plain cylinder sized by inner or outer diameter, the same way as the bottom end.

Printing

  • Download STL saves a file ready to slice. It is rendered by OpenSCAD on this computer, so run python3 generate.py --serve and open the address it prints. Download SCAD saves the source instead.
  • Circles are cut into flats 0.25 mm wide, finer than a 0.4 mm nozzle line, so a Bambu H2D prints them round.
  • Optimize for 3D printing adds 0.2 mm of clearance on every sized diameter, including a top tube and the Dyson outer diameter. An inner diameter is printed 0.2 mm larger, an outer diameter 0.2 mm smaller. It also cuts the stopper’s outer corners at 45° to its horizontal top and bottom edges so the overhang needs no support.
  • A stopper is a ridge in the transition: inside, outside, or both. Set its length along the axis and its height out from the wall. On a bend the outer shoulder runs farther than the inner one.
  • Print with the bottom tube on the bed and the top mouth facing up. A straight adapter, centered or along one edge, does not need supports. A bend may need support inside the elbow.
  • PETG is a good default. Use three or four perimeters so the wall stays airtight.
  • If a CLEANTEC plug will not turn, raise lip_deep_r and lip_shallow_r in the file by 0.1 mm or 0.2 mm.
