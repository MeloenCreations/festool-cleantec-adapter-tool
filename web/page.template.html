Festool CLEANTEC adapter

One end is the female CLEANTEC socket a Festool suction hose twists into. The other end is a tube you size. It can sit in the middle or along one edge, and it can leave the socket at an angle.

How it locks

Push the male CLEANTEC plug on the hose into the socket and twist it until the three lugs sit behind the shelves. Pulling straight out will not release it. Twist back to the entry slots to remove it.

Printing

  • Download STL saves a file ready to slice. It is rendered by OpenSCAD on this computer, so run python3 generate.py --serve and open the address it prints. Download SCAD saves the source instead.
  • Circles are cut into flats 0.25 mm wide, finer than a 0.4 mm nozzle line, so a Bambu H2D prints them round.
  • Optimize for 3D printing adds 0.2 mm of clearance on the diameter you chose. An inner diameter is printed 0.2 mm larger, an outer diameter 0.2 mm smaller.
  • Print with the tube on the bed and the socket mouth facing up. A straight adapter, centered or along one edge, does not need supports. A bend may need support inside the elbow.
  • PETG is a good default. Use three or four perimeters so the wall stays airtight.
  • If the plug will not turn, raise lip_deep_r and lip_shallow_r in the file by 0.1 mm or 0.2 mm.
